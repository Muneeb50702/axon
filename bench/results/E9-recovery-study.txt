These are BOUNDS, not the device number. The ceiling is the recovery the
loop permits when the model proposes a workable alternative; the floor is
what happens when it repeats itself, which E18 measured a 1B planner doing
three times in a row. The real rate depends on the model and needs a phone
(E9b). What the floor shows is that a non-recovering planner ESCALATES
rather than looping — the repetition guard converting a hang into an exit.
