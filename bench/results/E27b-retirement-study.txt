E27b — SKILL RETIREMENT THRESHOLD, CALIBRATION
================================================================================
rule: retire when replays >= 3 and clean rate < 0.5
retirement is ABSORBING — a retired skill is never replayed again

true clean rate   P(retired ≤10)   P(retired ≤50)   median replays to retire
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
95%               0.7%             0.7%             —
90%               3.1%             3.1%             —
80%               12.7%            12.9%            —
70%               28.6%            30.8%            —
60%               48.5%            57.6%            11
50%               68.5%            85.9%            3
40%               84.7%            98.5%            3
30%               94.7%            99.9%            3
20%               99.0%            99.9%            3
10%               99.9%            99.9%            3
================================================================================

A skill that replays cleanly 90% of the time is retired with probability
3.1% within 50 replays. Retirement is permanent until the goal
is cold-planned twice and recompiled, so that is the cost of the rule on
a skill that was working.

A genuinely rotten skill (30% clean) is retired with probability
99.9% within 50 replays, median 3 replays.

WHERE THE RISK LIVES. Almost all of it is at the FIRST judgement:
at 90% clean, P(retired at replay 3) is 2.7% of the
3.1% lifetime total. The minimum-sample constant is
therefore the lever, not the rate threshold:

  min replays   P(retire | 95%)   P(retire | 90%)   P(retire | 80%)   P(retire | 30%)
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3             0.7%              3.1%              12.9%             99.9%
  4             0.1%              0.9%              7.2%              99.9%
  5             0.1%              0.9%              7.2%              99.9%
  6             0.0%              0.3%              4.1%              99.9%
  7             0.0%              0.3%              4.1%              99.9%

WHY THE CONSTANT IS NOT CHANGED HERE. Raising it looks free above -- rotten
skills are still caught 99.9% of the time -- but that column hides the
cost. A higher minimum means a rotten skill is REPLAYED MORE TIMES before
retirement, and for a rotten skill most replays need repair, and a repaired
replay costs a planner call: ~60 s on this hardware (E2), not the ~2.3 s a
clean replay costs (E22d).

  going 3 -> 6 saves  (3.1% - 0.3%) x ~120 s re-learn  =  ~3 s per healthy skill
  and costs           ~3 extra replays x ~0.7 repair x ~60 s  =  ~126 s per rotten one

So the optimum depends entirely on the ratio of healthy to rotten skills in
a real store -- which is the SAME missing number E26c needs. The lever is
identified and priced; choosing its value is not possible from this study,
and picking one anyway would be exactly the unearned calibration E27
shipped with.
